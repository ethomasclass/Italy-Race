Mille Miglia game · Prototype

His Father's Car

Leg one of eight. Hill road south of Siena, May 1984.

Two brothers drive their late father's car along the old Mille Miglia route. There is no goal and no way to fail. The road runs 1.8 km, the car wallows, and the odometer starts on his mileage rather than theirs.
